HOUSE RULES

How to play Gay Chess

  1. Two kings, no queen. Each queen is replaced by a second king.
  2. Keep one alive. A king may be captured; the match continues if its partner survives.
  3. Protect both kings when possible. You may only leave a king in check when no move can save both kings. With one king left, normal check rules apply.
  4. Each king can castle with either rook. Long castling ends on b/c for the d-file king and c/d for the e-file king; short castling ends on f/e for the d-file king and g/f for the e-file king. Normal path and check restrictions apply.
  5. No new queens. Pawns promote to a rook, bishop, or knight.
  6. Draws. Stalemate, threefold repetition, and the fifty-move rule are draws.
